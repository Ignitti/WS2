Our Story

Crafting luxury candles for the modern gentleman since 2020. Every flame tells a story of sophistication, quality, and masculine elegance.

The Beginning

Ignitti was born from a simple observation: the candle market was dominated by floral and sweet scents, leaving discerning men without options that truly reflected their sophisticated tastes.

Founded in 2020 by master perfumer Alexander Chen, Ignitti represents a revolution in masculine fragrance. Each candle is meticulously crafted using premium soy wax and complex scent profiles that evoke confidence, luxury, and refinement.

From our workshop in Portland, Oregon, we hand-pour every candle with precision and care. Our signature scents include notes of aged whiskey, cedar, leather, tobacco, and exotic spices—creating an olfactory experience that transforms any space into a gentleman's sanctuary.

Alexander Chen

Master Perfumer & Founder

With over 15 years of experience in luxury fragrance development, Alexander Chen has worked with some of the world's most prestigious perfume houses before founding Ignitti.

His passion for creating sophisticated, masculine scents led him to identify a gap in the market for high-quality candles designed specifically for the modern gentleman.

"Every man deserves a signature scent that reflects his character, ambition, and sophistication. That's what we create at Ignitti."

Today, Alexander continues to personally oversee every aspect of fragrance development, ensuring that each Ignitti candle meets the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.
